# Acme Robotics Handbook

## Support hours

Customer support operates Monday through Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 Europe/Berlin time. Critical production incidents may be reported through the on-call channel at any time.

## Returns

Hardware may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The product must include its original accessories. Customized hardware is not eligible for return unless it is defective.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Team leads approve recurring remote schedules. Employees handling laboratory hardware must be onsite whenever physical testing is scheduled.

## Security incidents

Suspected credential exposure must be reported to the security team immediately. Affected credentials must be rotated; they must never be pasted into tickets or chat messages.
